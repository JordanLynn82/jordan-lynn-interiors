How It Works

Four simple steps

  1. Walkthrough

    I visit your space and we talk through what you have in mind.

  2. Inspiration

    Provide photos of looks you love.

    Don't have an idea? I'll create a proposal for you.

  3. Order the Pieces

    I order the décor you approve, based on your inspiration or my proposal.

  4. Install Day

    I bring everything over, then place and arrange it all.

No vision yet? No problem.Tell me about your space and your style, and I'll put together a proposal for you to approve.
