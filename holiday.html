Holiday Styling

Holiday, Styled.

Seasonal decorating for homes and local businesses on Long Beach Island and across South Jersey. I design the look, bring everything, install it, and come back to take it all down when the season's over.

Now booking the 2026 season. Book by December 1 to have everything up in time for Christmas.

Residential

For your Home

Come home to a house that feels like the holidays, without a single trip to the attic.

  • Trees — designed, lit and decorated, real or faux
  • Mantels & staircases — layered garland, stockings and candlelight
  • Front doors & porches — wreaths, planters and entry greenery
  • Tablescapes — ready-to-host tables for Thanksgiving and holiday dinners
  • Shore houses — festive and ready before you arrive for the holidays
Commercial

For your Business

A beautifully dressed space draws people in. I work around your hours so you never have to close.

  • Storefront windows — displays that stop people on the sidewalk
  • Restaurants & bars — dining rooms, bar tops and entryways
  • Offices & lobbies — a warm welcome for clients and staff
  • Exterior greenery — garland, wreaths and planters out front
  • Rentals & listings — holiday-ready staging for guests and showings
